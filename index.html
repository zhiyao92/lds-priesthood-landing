A few useful answers.

Is this an official Church app?

No. It is an independent study and reference aid and is not an official app of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

Does an app give me authority to perform an ordinance?

No. Follow current official Church guidance and the direction of authorized leaders concerning authority, permission and procedure.

Which features require Pro?

Guided and focus modes, read-aloud, all available languages, an unlimited journal, custom themes and the ad-free experience are listed as Pro features.

Can I search and save a reference?

Yes. The released description includes search across ordinances, prayers, offices and topics, plus favorites for frequently used material.
